💡 My Experience: Logic Building, Programming, and Data Structures at LPU

I recently had the opportunity to participate in a skill enhancement program organized by the Centre for Professional Enhancement at Lovely Professional University, titled:
"Logic Building, Programming and Data Structures: Learn How to Think, & Program."

This course, held from June 5, 2024 to July 15, 2024, was an incredible experience that not only strengthened my programming fundamentals but also reshaped the way I approach problem-solving.

Throughout the course, I explored:

• Foundational logic building techniques
• Core data structures like arrays, stacks, queues, and linked lists
• Real-world programming challenges that sharpened my analytical thinking

What stood out to me most was the "learn how to think" aspect—encouraging structured thinking before jumping into code. This shift in mindset helped me write cleaner, more efficient programs.

I'm proud to have received an A Grade for my performance, and this recognition has boosted my confidence to take on more complex programming tasks in future projects.
